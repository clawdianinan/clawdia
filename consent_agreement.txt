CONSENT AGREEMENT
Kwara State Government (“KWSG”) and IHS (Nigeria) Limited (“IHS”) entered into a collaboration
agreement dated 1st January 2025 for the construction, operation and execution of programs at the Ilorin
Innovation Hub located at Ilorin, Kwara State. IHS in furtherance of its sustainability strategy, intends to
build innovation hubs across certain locations within Nigeria and has requested KWSG’s consent to use
the logo and brand initials as provided in the schedule (the “Derivative Marks”), together with all
associated goodwill and intellectual property rights for the branding, promotion and operation of IHS
innovation hubs across Nigeria. KWSG hereby grants IHS an unlimited and exclusive right to use the
Derivative Marks in accordance with the following terms and conditions:
1.
KWSG hereby consents and authorizes IHS to use the Derivative Marks for the branding,
promotion and operation of IHS innovation hubs across Nigeria.
2.
IHS shall have exclusive, unlimited and perpetual rights to use, publish, develop, modify, license
and register the Derivative Marks in connection with the IHS innovation hubs.
3.
This consent is granted in perpetuity and shall survive any change in the ownership and
management of either party.
4.
This agreement shall not be construed as a partnership, agency, representative between the
parties.
5.
This agreement shall be binding on both parties and their respective successors-in title and
assigns.
6.
This agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Federal
Republic of Nigeria.

Signed for and on behalf of both parties:

For: Kwara State Government

For: IHS (Nigeria) Limited
