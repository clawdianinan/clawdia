## 2. Programs & Events Report

### Month Overview
In February, the Programmes function prioritized ecosystem growth, founder resilience, and practical talent development while maintaining structured execution support for ventures in pre-incubation, incubation, and accelerator phases.

### Program Goal
The month’s programme objective was to strengthen IIH’s venture and talent pipelines through practical interventions, deepen ecosystem participation, and move supported ventures closer to measurable implementation outcomes.

### Key Activities Conducted This Month
- Builder’s Path fireside engagement focused on entrepreneurial resilience and recovery from business setbacks.
- InnovateAI Summit (Ilorin Experience Centre), enabling local participants to connect to the national summit ecosystem.
- Continued implementation of Learn2Earn Fellowship, Pre-Incubation & Incubation Programme, and IIH Accelerator support activities.

### Detailed Programme Narrative
Under ecosystem development, the Builder’s Path session drew 22 participants and strengthened peer exchange around long-term execution resilience.

Within the Animate Africa 3D track, 279 initial enrollees narrowed to approximately 50 active learners based on attendance and milestone thresholds. An AMA session with 12 active learners highlighted clear delivery feedback: participants requested more frequent physical sessions and stronger on-site tutor support. Participation behaviour in this stream is being reassessed for improved completion outcomes.

For venture support, 9 pre-incubation ventures and 5 incubation-stage startups continued curriculum progression. Session schedules were adjusted to evening windows to improve founder attendance. Following panel review of milestone and funding-use submissions, revised implementation plans were submitted and are under evaluation.

In talent development, Learn2Earn onboarded 171 fellows into foundational modules (including structured prompting and introductory programming pathways) as part of the 24-month fellowship cycle. Early-stage engagement support included daily meals during the first month to stabilize attendance and routine.

In acceleration, 9 startups progressed toward Demo Day and pilot-readiness. Eight ventures are preparing pilot engagements with IHS. Notable execution examples included operational validation visits (enee.io, Gauge), external deployment readiness (Insight Works, Flowsoft), and solution piloting discussions (Movvn, POD, SPCTA). PERWER, GoChargeAm, POD, and Gauge participated in demo exposure at the Africa Deep Tech Foundation Conference.

IIH also progressed grassroots ecosystem mobilization through DBN ETP 8.0 outreach, reaching 171 MSME registrations to date.

### Program Metrics Summary
- Talent Training target: 100 | actual: 171 | variance: +71
- Virtual attendance target: 33% | actual: 71% | variance: +38%
- Physical attendance target: 50% | actual: 48% | variance: -2%
- Overall registrations reported: 512
- Combined monthly attendance (physical + virtual): 253
- Registration-to-attendance ratio: approximately 2:1

### Programme Challenges and Mitigation
- Lower sustained participation in selected hybrid technical streams; mitigation includes delivery redesign toward higher physical support.
- Physical attendance marginally below target; ongoing session design and scheduling optimization underway.
- Continued need for milestone quality control in early-stage venture execution; programme review and guidance loops have been strengthened.

### Next-Month Program Priorities
- Execute planned CcHUB activities (Ilorin DevBox and HERizon).
- Sustain Learn2Earn progression and attendance quality.
- Move accelerator ventures from readiness to pilot implementation with IHS.
- Continue DBN ETP 8.0 mobilization-to-delivery transition.
