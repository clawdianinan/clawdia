## Executive Summary
Based on the email from IHS Towers requesting consent for logo usage, I recommend **caution and careful negotiation**. The request raises significant branding and ownership concerns that need to be addressed before granting consent.

## Key Issues Identified

### 1. **Ownership & Brand Identity Conflict**
- **Fact:** IIH is owned by Kwara State Government, not IHS Towers
- **Risk:** Allowing IHS to use similar branding for Maiduguri Hub creates confusion about ownership and control
- **Concern:** This could dilute IIH's independent identity and create perception that IIH is an IHS subsidiary

### 2. **Legal & Governance Implications**
- **Consent Agreement Scope:** Need to review the specific terms of the attached consent agreement
- **Rights Granted:** What exclusive/non-exclusive rights are being requested?
- **Duration & Termination:** Is this permanent or time-limited? What are termination clauses?
- **Modification Rights:** Who controls future logo modifications?

### 3. **Strategic Considerations**
- **Partnership vs. Subordination:** The arrangement should reflect partnership, not subordination
- **Brand Hierarchy:** Need clear visual differentiation between IIH and Maiduguri Hub logos
- **Future Expansion:** Sets precedent for other potential hubs

## Recommended Response Strategy

1. **Request Clarification:** Ask IHS for the strategic rationale behind identical branding
2. **Review Agreement:** Have legal counsel review the consent agreement terms
3. **Define Boundaries:** Establish clear branding guidelines for the relationship
