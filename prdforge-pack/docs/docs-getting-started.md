# Getting Started with PRDForge

Welcome to PRDForge! This guide will help you get up and running quickly with our AI-powered PRD (Product Requirements Document) generation platform.

## Welcome to PRDForge

PRDForge is designed to help product managers, founders, and teams create comprehensive product requirements documents faster and more effectively. Our AI-powered platform guides you through the PRD creation process, ensuring you capture all essential details while maintaining consistency and clarity.

## Account Setup and Verification

### Creating Your Account
1. Visit [app.prdforge.com](https://app.prdforge.com)
2. Click "Sign Up" in the top right corner
3. Choose your preferred sign-up method:
   - Email and password
   - Google account
   - GitHub account
4. Complete the registration form

### Email Verification
1. Check your email for a verification link
2. Click the link to verify your account
3. You'll be redirected to the PRDForge dashboard

### Setting Up Your Profile
1. Click on your profile picture in the top right
2. Select "Profile Settings"
3. Add your:
   - Full name
   - Job title
   - Company (optional)
   - Profile picture (optional)
4. Click "Save Changes"

## Understanding the Dashboard

### Navigation Overview
- **Dashboard**: Overview of your projects and activity
- **Projects**: Manage all your PRD projects
- **Templates**: Browse and select PRD templates
- **Credits**: View and manage your credit balance
- **Settings**: Account and billing settings

### Dashboard Components
1. **Quick Stats**
   - Active projects
   - Credits remaining
   - Recent activity

2. **Recent Projects**
   - Your most recent PRDs
   - Status indicators (Draft, In Progress, Complete)
   - Quick access buttons

3. **Activity Feed**
   - Recent generations
   - Team updates (if on team plan)
   - System announcements

## Your First PRD in 5 Minutes

### Step 1: Create a New Project
1. Click the "New Project" button
2. Enter a project name
3. Select a template (or start from scratch)
4. Click "Create Project"

### Step 2: Define Your Product
1. **Product Name**: Enter your product's name
2. **Description**: Briefly describe what your product does
3. **Target Audience**: Who will use this product?
4. **Problem Statement**: What problem does it solve?

### Step 3: Generate Core Sections
1. Click "Generate with AI" for each section:
   - **Features & Requirements**
   - **User Stories**
   - **Technical Requirements**
   - **Success Metrics**
2. Review and edit the AI-generated content
3. Add your own insights and details

### Step 4: Review and Finalize
1. Use the "Review" tab to check completeness
2. Run the "Quality Check" tool
3. Share with stakeholders for feedback
4. Export your PRD when ready

### Step 5: Export Your PRD
1. Click the "Export" button
2. Choose your format:
   - PDF (for sharing)
   - Word Document (.docx)
   - Markdown (.md)
   - JSON (for integration)
   - CSV (for data analysis)
   - Google Sheets (collaboration)
3. Download or share directly
