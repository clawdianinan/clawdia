### 1.2 Sales Process Stages

**Stage 1: Prospecting & Outreach**
- **Goal:** Schedule discovery call
- **Activities:** Email sequences, LinkedIn outreach, content engagement
- **Success Metric:** 20% response rate, 10% meeting booked

**Stage 2: Discovery Call**
- **Goal:** Understand pain points, qualify fit
- **Activities:** Needs analysis, demo preview, next steps
- **Success Metric:** 60% conversion to demo

**Stage 3: Demo & Solution Presentation**
- **Goal:** Show value, address objections
- **Activities:** Customized demo, ROI calculation, social proof
- **Success Metric:** 40% conversion to proposal

**Stage 4: Proposal & Negotiation**
- **Goal:** Close deal with favorable terms
- **Activities:** Proposal delivery, negotiation, legal review
- **Success Metric:** 70% close rate

**Stage 5: Onboarding & Expansion**
- **Goal:** Successful implementation, identify expansion opportunities
- **Activities:** Kickoff call, training, quarterly business reviews
- **Success Metric:** 90% retention, 30% expansion

### 1.3 Qualification Framework (BANT)

**Budget:**
- **Questions:**
  - "What's your current budget for productivity tools?"
  - "How do you typically purchase software (credit card, PO, etc.)?"
  - "What's your approval process for new tools?"
- **Qualified:** Has budget authority or clear approval path

**Authority:**
- **Questions:**
  - "Who else is involved in this decision?"
  - "What's your role in the evaluation process?"
  - "Who needs to sign off on this purchase?"
- **Qualified:** Decision maker or strong influencer

**Need:**
- **Questions:**
  - "How much time does your team spend on PRDs currently?"
  - "What are the biggest challenges with your current process?"
  - "What would solving this problem enable you to do?"
- **Qualified:** Clear pain points that PRDForge solves

**Timeline:**
- **Questions:**
  - "When are you looking to implement a solution?"
  - "What's driving this timeline?"
  - "Are there any upcoming projects that require better documentation?"
- **Qualified:** Decision within 30-90 days

### 1.4 Objection Handling Guide

**Objection 1: "We're happy with our current process"**
- **Response:** "That's great to hear! Many teams start out thinking their process works well until they see how much time they're actually spending. Can I ask how many hours per week your team spends on PRDs currently?"
- **Follow-up:** "If we could cut that time by 85% while improving quality, would that be worth exploring?"

**Objection 2: "It's too expensive"**
- **Response:** "I understand budget is important. Let's look at the ROI - if your product team saves 17 hours per week at an average rate of $[amount]/hour, that's $[amount] in weekly savings. PRDForge pays for itself in [timeframe]."
- **Alternative:** "We have a Starter plan at $12/month that might fit your budget better. Would you like to see that option?"

**Objection 3: "We need to think about it"**
- **Response:** "Absolutely, I encourage thoughtful decisions. What specific information would help you make a decision? Is it about features, pricing, implementation, or something else?"
- **Next step:** "Can we schedule a follow-up for [specific date] to address those questions?"

**Objection 4: "We already use [competitor]"**
- **Response:** "That's interesting! What do you like about [competitor]? What challenges are you still facing with it?"
- **Differentiation:** "PRDForge differs in [specific ways]. Would you be open to a quick comparison to see if we might be a better fit?"

**Objection 5: "We don't have time to implement new tools"**
- **Response:** "I understand implementation time is a concern. PRDForge is designed for quick setup - most teams are creating their first PRD within 15 minutes. Our onboarding team can help get you started quickly."
- **Social proof:** "[Similar company] was up and running in under an hour and saved 20 hours in their first week."

### 1.5 Closing Techniques

**Assumptive Close:**
- "Based on what we've discussed, the Pro plan seems like the best fit. Shall we get started with the implementation next week?"

**Alternative Choice Close:**
- "Would you prefer to start with the annual plan to save 20%, or would the monthly plan work better for your budgeting?"

**Summary Close:**
- "Let me summarize what we've agreed on: You need to reduce PRD time by 85%, improve team alignment, and have this implemented within 30 days. PRDForge addresses all three. Are you ready to move forward?"

**Trial Close:**
- "If we could address [specific concern], would you be ready to move forward?"

**Urgency Close:**
- "Our launch promotion of 20% off ends this Friday. Would you like to lock in that rate?"

### 2.2 30-Minute Demo Script

**Introduction (5 minutes)**
- "Thanks for joining! Today I'll show you how PRDForge can help your team create PRDs 10x faster."
- "Quick agenda: 1) The problem we solve, 2) Live demo, 3) How it works for teams like yours, 4) Q&A"
- "Before we start, can you share a bit about your current PRD process and biggest challenges?"

**Problem Framing (3 minutes)**
- "Most product teams we work with spend 20+ hours per week on PRDs."
- "Common pain points: inconsistent formats, version chaos, team misalignment."
- "The result: delayed launches, frustrated teams, missed opportunities."

**Live Demo (15 minutes)**

**Part 1: AI-Powered Generation (5 minutes)**
- "Let me show you the core magic. I'll describe a product idea in plain English..."
- [Demonstrate natural language input → structured PRD]
- "Notice how the AI structures requirements, adds acceptance criteria, and suggests best practices."

**Part 2: Collaboration Features (5 minutes)**
- "Now let's invite team members to collaborate..."
- [Demonstrate real-time editing, comments, version history]
- "This eliminates email chains and version control issues."

**Part 3: Export & Integration (3 minutes)**
- "When you're ready to share..."
- [Demonstrate one-click export to Word, PDF, Confluence]
- "Everything stays in sync with your existing tools."

**Part 4: Templates & Customization (2 minutes)**
- "We have 20+ industry templates, but you can also create your own..."
- [Show template library and customization options]
- "This ensures consistency across all your PRDs."

**Tailored Value Proposition (5 minutes)**
- "Based on what you shared earlier, here's how PRDForge would help your team specifically..."
- [Connect features to their stated pain points]
- "Teams like [similar company] saved 85% time and improved alignment."

**Q&A & Next Steps (7 minutes)**
- "What questions do you have about what you've seen?"
- "Based on this, does PRDForge seem like it could address your challenges?"
- "Next steps would be [proposal, trial, implementation plan]."

### 2.3 Demo Best Practices

**Do:**
- Keep it conversational, not monologue
- Focus on benefits, not just features
- Use their language and examples
- Check for understanding frequently
- Handle objections as they arise

**Don't:**
- Rush through the demo
- Show every feature (focus on relevant ones)
- Make assumptions without confirming
- Overpromise or exaggerate
- Forget to ask for next steps

### 2.4 Post-Demo Follow-up

**Immediate (within 1 hour):**
- Send thank you email with:
  - Key points discussed
  - Customized proposal if ready
  - Next steps and timeline
  - Additional resources (case studies, ROI calculator)

**24-Hour Follow-up:**
- Check if they have additional questions
- Share relevant customer story
- Offer extended trial or pilot

**3-Day Follow-up:**
- If no response, gentle nudge with new value prop
- Offer alternative contact method (call, different time)
- Consider involving manager if appropriate

## SECTION 4: PRICING JUSTIFICATION DOCUMENT

### 4.1 ROI Calculator

**Input Variables:**
- Number of product team members: [ ]
- Average hourly rate: $[ ]/hour
- Hours spent on PRDs per week: [ ]
- Weeks per year: 52

**Calculation:**
1. **Current Annual Cost:**
   - Team members × hours/week × hourly rate × 52 weeks
   - Example: 3 PMs × 20 hours × $50/hour × 52 = $156,000

2. **PRDForge Cost:**
   - Pro plan: $24/user/month × 3 users × 12 months = $864/year

3. **Time Savings (Conservative):**
   - 70% time reduction (vs. 85% average)
   - $156,000 × 70% = $109,200 saved

4. **Net Savings:**
   - $109,200 - $864 = $108,336 annual savings

5. **ROI:**
   - ($108,336 / $864) × 100 = 12,540% ROI

6. **Payback Period:**
   - Less than 1 week
