## SECTION 3: FAQ DOCUMENTATION

### 3.1 Getting Started FAQs

**Q: How do I create my first PRD?**
A: Click "New PRD" from your dashboard, describe your product idea in plain English, choose a template, and let AI generate the structure. Then customize with your specific details.

**Q: What should I write in the description box?**
A: Describe your product or feature in simple terms. Example: "A dashboard for tracking team productivity with weekly reports and goal tracking."

**Q: How do I choose the right template?**
A: Browse templates by industry (SaaS, Mobile, Enterprise) or use case (New Feature, Product Launch, Improvement). You can always switch templates later.

**Q: Can I edit the AI-generated content?**
A: Absolutely! The AI provides a structured starting point. You should review and customize every section with your specific requirements.

**Q: How do I save my work?**
A: PRDForge auto-saves every 30 seconds. You can also manually save using Ctrl+S (Cmd+S on Mac) or the save button.

### 3.2 Collaboration FAQs

**Q: How do I invite team members?**
A: Open any PRD, click "Share" in the top right, enter email addresses, and set permission levels (can edit or can view).

**Q: Can multiple people edit at the same time?**
A: Yes! PRDForge supports real-time collaboration. You'll see teammates' cursors and changes as they happen.

**Q: How do comments work?**
A: Highlight any text and click the comment icon. Comments create threads that can be resolved when addressed.

**Q: Is there version history?**
A: Yes! Click "History" in the top menu to see all changes, who made them, and when. You can restore previous versions.

**Q: How do approval workflows work?**
A: Mark a PRD as "Ready for Review" to notify approvers. They can comment, request changes, or approve.

### 3.3 Export & Integration FAQs

**Q: What formats can I export to?**
A: Microsoft Word (.docx), PDF, HTML, and direct Confluence push.

**Q: How do I export to Confluence?**
A: First, connect your Confluence account in Settings → Integrations. Then click "Export" → "Push to Confluence" from any PRD.

**Q: Can I use my own Word template?**
A: Yes! Upload your company Word template in Settings → Templates. All exports will use your branding.

**Q: Is there an API?**
A: Yes, Pro and Enterprise plans include API access. Documentation at [api.prdforge.com](link).

**Q: Can I import existing PRDs?**
A: Currently, you can copy-paste content from Word/Google Docs. Bulk import is coming soon.

### 3.4 Billing & Account FAQs

**Q: What happens when my trial ends?**
A: You'll revert to the Free plan (3 PRDs/month, basic features). Upgrade before trial ends to keep Pro features.

**Q: How do I upgrade/downgrade?**
A: Go to Settings → Billing and select your plan. Changes take effect immediately.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for annual plans. Monthly plans can be canceled anytime.

**Q: How do I cancel my subscription?**
A: Go to Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription. You'll retain access until the end of your billing period.

**Q: Do you offer educational/non-profit discounts?**
A: Yes! Contact support with proof of status for special pricing.

### 3.5 Technical FAQs

**Q: What browsers are supported?**
A: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest versions). We recommend Chrome for best performance.

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**
A: Not yet, but our web app is fully responsive and works great on mobile browsers.

**Q: How is my data secured?**
A: All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). We're GDPR compliant and undergo regular security audits.

**Q: Where are your servers located?**
A: AWS us-east-1 (North Virginia) with backups in us-west-2 (Oregon).

**Q: What's your uptime SLA?**
A: 99.9% for Pro plans, 99.95% for Enterprise. Status page at [status.prdforge.com](link).

### 3.6 Advanced Features FAQs

**Q: How do custom templates work?**
A: Create a PRD with your desired structure, then save it as a template. Your team can then use it for new PRDs.

**Q: Can I automate PRD creation?**
A: Yes, using our API or Zapier integration (coming soon). Automate from Jira tickets, user feedback, or meeting notes.

**Q: How does the AI learn my preferences?**
A: The AI analyzes your edits and approvals to suggest better content over time. You can reset this in Settings → AI Preferences.

**Q: Are there keyboard shortcuts?**
A: Yes! Press ? in the editor to see all shortcuts. Common ones: Ctrl+B for bold, Ctrl+I for italic, Ctrl+K for link.

**Q: Can I set up custom fields?**
A: Enterprise plans include custom field configuration. Contact sales to discuss your needs.

## SECTION 4: SUPPORT ESCALATION MATRIX

### 4.1 Support Tiers & Response Times

**Free Plan: Community Support**
- **Channels:** Help Center, Community Forum
- **Response Time:** 48-72 hours
- **Scope:** Basic troubleshooting, documentation questions
- **Escalation Path:** None (self-service only)

**Starter Plan: Email Support**
- **Channels:** Email, Help Center
- **Response Time:** 24 hours (business hours)
- **Scope:** Account issues, feature questions, basic troubleshooting
- **Escalation Path:** Email → Support Lead (48h)

**Pro Plan: Priority Support**
- **Channels:** Email, Live Chat (business hours), Help Center
- **Response Time:** 4 hours (business hours)
- **Scope:** Technical issues, integration help, best practices
- **Escalation Path:** Chat → Senior Support → Engineering (24h)

**Enterprise Plan: Dedicated Support**
- **Channels:** Dedicated Slack channel, Phone, Email, Live Chat
- **Response Time:** 1 hour (24/7 for P1 issues)
- **Scope:** Everything + custom configurations, training, strategic guidance
- **Escalation Path:** Direct to assigned Customer Success Manager

### 4.2 Issue Severity Levels

**P1: Critical (Service Down)**
- **Examples:** Cannot login, data loss, security breach
- **Response:** < 1 hour (24/7)
- **Resolution Target:** < 4 hours
- **Communication:** Hourly updates, status page
- **Escalation:** Immediate to Engineering Director

**P2: High (Major Functionality Broken)**
- **Examples:** Cannot create PRDs, export failing, collaboration broken
- **Response:** < 4 hours (business hours)
- **Resolution Target:** < 24 hours
- **Communication:** Daily updates
- **Escalation:** Support Lead → Engineering (8h)

**P3: Medium (Minor Functionality Issue)**
- **Examples:** UI bugs, slow performance, feature not working as expected
- **Response:** < 24 hours
- **Resolution Target:** < 1 week
- **Communication:** Upon resolution
- **Escalation:** Support → Product (48h)

**P4: Low (Enhancement Request)**
- **Examples:** New feature requests, UI improvements, documentation suggestions
- **Response:** < 72 hours
- **Resolution Target:** Product roadmap consideration
- **Communication:** Monthly product update
- **Escalation:** Product feedback system

### 4.3 Escalation Procedures

**Step 1: Initial Contact**
- Customer submits issue through appropriate channel
- Support agent categorizes severity (P1-P4)
- Initial response within SLA timeframe
- Issue tracking number provided

**Step 2: Investigation & Diagnosis**
- Support agent attempts reproduction
- Gathers logs, screenshots, system info
- Checks known issues and workarounds
- Updates customer on progress

**Step 3: Escalation Decision**
- If unresolved after [timeframe based on severity]:
  - P1: Immediate escalation to engineering
  - P2: Escalate after 4 hours of investigation
  - P3: Escalate if no workaround after 24h
  - P4: Add to product backlog

**Step 4: Engineering Involvement**
- Support provides complete case details
- Engineering investigates root cause
- Provides fix timeline or workaround
- Support communicates to customer

**Step 5: Resolution & Follow-up**
- Fix deployed or workaround provided
- Customer confirms resolution
- Case documented for knowledge base
- Follow-up survey sent

### 4.4 On-call Rotation

**Primary On-call (Engineering):**
- **Schedule:** Weekly rotation
- **Hours:** 24/7 for P1 issues
- **Responsibilities:** Immediate response to P1 escalations
- **Backup:** Secondary on-call engineer

**Secondary On-call (Support):**
- **Schedule:** Daily rotation (business hours)
- **Hours:** 9 AM - 6 PM local time
- **Responsibilities:** P2 escalations, customer communication
- **Backup:** Support lead

**Management Escalation:**
- **Support Manager:** Available for customer escalations
- **Engineering Manager:** Technical decision authority
- **Head of Customer Success:** Strategic account issues
- **CEO:** Critical business-impacting issues

### 4.5 Communication Protocols

**Status Page Updates:**
- **P1 Issues:** Updates every 30 minutes
- **P2 Issues:** Updates every 4 hours
- **P3/P4:** Weekly maintenance window notifications

**Customer Communication:**
- **Initial:** Acknowledgement with ticket number

**Internal Communication:**

## SECTION 5: CUSTOMER SUCCESS FRAMEWORK

### 5.1 Success Metrics Definition

**Adoption Metrics:**
- **Time to First PRD:** Target < 15 minutes
- **Weekly Active Users:** Target > 70% of team
- **PRDs Created per User:** Target > 2/week
- **Feature Adoption Rate:** Target > 50% for core features

**Engagement Metrics:**
- **Session Duration:** Target > 10 minutes
- **Return Rate:** Target > 3 sessions/week
- **Collaboration Rate:** Target > 30% of PRDs with multiple editors
- **Export Rate:** Target > 60% of PRDs exported

**Business Value Metrics:**
- **Time Saved:** Self-reported hours saved/week
- **Quality Improvement:** Reduction in requirement-related bugs
- **Team Alignment:** Survey scores on collaboration effectiveness
- **ROI Realization:** Comparison of savings vs. cost

### 5.2 Health Scoring System

**Health Score Components (0-100):**
1. **Usage (40 points):**
   - PRDs created in last 30 days (20 points)
   - Team members active (10 points)
   - Feature adoption (10 points)

2. **Engagement (30 points):**
   - Session frequency (10 points)
   - Collaboration activity (10 points)
   - Support interactions (10 points)

3. **Value Realization (30 points):**
   - ROI survey score (15 points)
   - NPS/CSAT scores (10 points)
   - Expansion potential (5 points)

**Health Tiers:**
- **Healthy (80-100):** High engagement, realizing value
- **Needs Attention (60-79):** Moderate usage, some risk factors
- **At Risk (40-59):** Low engagement, not realizing value
- **Critical (0-39):** Inactive, likely to churn

### 5.3 Proactive Outreach Triggers

**Positive Triggers (Celebration & Expansion):**
- Created 10+ PRDs in first month
- Invited 5+ team members
- Set up Confluence integration
- High health score (>80)
- **Action:** Success story request, feature recommendations, referral ask

**Neutral Triggers (Education & Engagement):**
- Created 1-2 PRDs then inactive
- Low collaboration activity
- Not using key features
- Medium health score (60-79)
- **Action:** Feature education, best practices, webinar invitation

**Negative Triggers (Risk Mitigation):**
- Trial ending with low activity
- Support tickets unresolved
- Negative feedback
- Low health score (<60)
- **Action:** Personalized check-in, troubleshooting, special offer
