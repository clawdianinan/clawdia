## SECTION 5: RISK MANAGEMENT & CONTINGENCY PLANS

### 5.1 Identified Risks

**High Probability, High Impact:**
1. **Technical Issues:** System downtime during launch
2. **Poor Conversion:** Lower than expected signup-to-paid conversion
3. **Negative Feedback:** Public criticism affecting reputation

**Medium Probability, High Impact:**
4. **Competitor Response:** Competitors launch similar features or campaigns
5. **Team Burnout:** Excessive workload leading to errors or attrition
6. **Budget Overrun:** Marketing spend exceeds ROI

**Low Probability, High Impact:**
7. **Security Breach:** Data breach or security incident
8. **Legal Issues:** Compliance or regulatory problems
9. **Market Shift:** External factors affecting demand

### 5.2 Mitigation Strategies

**Technical Issues Mitigation:**
- **Prevention:** Load testing at 3x expected traffic, redundancy in critical systems
- **Detection:** 24/7 monitoring with automated alerts
- **Response:** War room with engineering on-call, rollback procedures documented
- **Communication:** Status page updates every 30 minutes during incidents

**Poor Conversion Mitigation:**
- **Prevention:** A/B testing of landing pages pre-launch, clear value proposition
- **Detection:** Real-time analytics dashboard, conversion funnel monitoring
- **Response:** Rapid iteration based on data, offer optimization
- **Communication:** Transparent about improvements being made

**Negative Feedback Mitigation:**
- **Prevention:** Thorough QA, user testing, clear documentation
- **Detection:** Social listening tools, review monitoring
- **Response:** Professional, helpful responses, issue resolution transparency
- **Communication:** Public commitment to improvement, regular updates

### 5.3 Contingency Plans

**If signups are below target (Day T+3):**
- **Action 1:** Increase paid advertising budget by 50%
- **Action 2:** Launch referral program with incentives
- **Action 3:** Partner with influencers for additional promotion
- **Action 4:** Offer extended trial period (21 days instead of 14)

**If conversion rate is below target (Day T+10):**
- **Action 1:** Implement exit-intent offers on pricing page
- **Action 2:** Add live chat for immediate sales assistance
- **Action 3:** Create limited-time launch discount
- **Action 4:** Personal outreach to trial users by sales team

**If system experiences downtime (Any time):**
- **Action 1:** Immediate engineering response (SLA: 15 minutes)
- **Action 2:** Status page updates every 15 minutes
- **Action 3:** Social media communication about issue and ETA
- **Action 4:** Compensation offer for affected users (extended trial)

### 5.4 Communication Protocols During Issues

**Internal Communication:**
- **Channel:** #launch-war-room Slack channel
- **Frequency:** Immediate updates during incidents
- **Decision Making:** Clear escalation path to leadership
- **Documentation:** All decisions and actions logged

**External Communication:**
- **Channel:** Status page (status.prdforge.com)
- **Frequency:** Every 30 minutes during incidents
- **Tone:** Transparent, professional, solution-focused
- **Follow-up:** Post-incident report and improvements

**Stakeholder Communication:**
- **Investors:** Daily summary during launch week, weekly thereafter
- **Team:** Daily standup during launch, weekly all-hands
- **Partners:** Weekly update email
- **Customers:** Monthly newsletter with launch results
