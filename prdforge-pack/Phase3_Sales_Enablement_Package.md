# Phase 3: Sales Enablement Package

## Sales Playbook

### Target Customer Profiles

**Ideal Customer Profile (ICP):**
- **Company Size:** 10-500 employees
- **Industry:** Technology, SaaS, FinTech, HealthTech
- **Role:** Product Managers, Product Owners, Technical Leads
- **Pain Points:**
  - Spending too much time on documentation
  - Inconsistent PRD quality
  - Difficulty aligning teams on requirements
  - Manual export and formatting issues
- **Budget:** $9-19/month per user
- **Decision Makers:** Head of Product, CTO, Engineering Manager

### Sales Process Stages

**Stage 1: Discovery (Days 1-3)**
- **Goal:** Identify pain points and needs
- **Activities:** Initial call, needs assessment, demo
- **Qualification Criteria:**
  - Has product team > 3 people
  - Creates > 5 PRDs per month
  - Currently using manual methods
  - Budget available

**Stage 2: Evaluation (Days 4-7)**
- **Goal:** Prove value and fit
- **Activities:** Technical deep dive, pilot setup, ROI calculation
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Key stakeholders engaged
  - Clear understanding of features
  - ROI calculation shows value
  - Technical feasibility confirmed

**Stage 3: Proposal (Days 8-10)**
- **Goal:** Present solution and pricing
- **Activities:** Proposal creation, pricing discussion, contract review
- **Documents Needed:**
  - Customized proposal
  - ROI analysis
  - Implementation timeline
  - Support plan

**Stage 4: Close (Days 11-14)**
- **Goal:** Secure commitment
- **Activities:** Contract signing, onboarding setup, payment processing
- **Success Metrics:**
  - Contract signed
  - First payment received
  - Onboarding scheduled

### Pricing Strategy

**Value-Based Pricing Approach:**
- **Free:** $0 (10 credits) - Acquisition tool
- **Starter:** $9/month (100 credits) - Growth revenue
- **Pro:** $19/month (300 credits) - Premium revenue

**Discount Strategy:**
- **Annual Discount:** 20% off for annual commitment
- **Team Discount:** 10% off for 5+ seats
- **Enterprise:** Custom pricing for 50+ seats

**Upsell Path:**
1. Free → Starter: "10x more credits for $9"
2. Starter → Pro: "3x more credits for 30% less per credit"
3. Pro → Enterprise: "Custom solutions for large teams"

### Competitive Positioning

**PRDForge vs. Manual Methods:**
- **Speed:** 10x faster
- **Quality:** AI ensures completeness
- **Consistency:** Templates maintain standards
- **Collaboration:** Real-time editing
- **Export:** One-click multiple formats

**PRDForge vs. Competitors:**
- **Price:** More affordable ($19 vs $29-49 competitors)
- **Credits:** More generous (300 vs 100-200 competitors)
- **Features:** Export included, API all tiers
- **Ease of Use:** Simpler interface, faster onboarding

## Objection Handling Guide

### Price Objections

**"It's too expensive."**
- **Response:** "Let's look at the ROI. If your team spends 20 hours/month on PRDs at $50/hour, that's $1,000/month. PRDForge at $19 saves $981/month."
- **Alternative:** "We offer annual billing with 20% discount, bringing it to $15.20/month."

**"We can do this manually for free."**
- **Response:** "True, but consider the opportunity cost. What could your team do with the 10+ hours/month saved? Also, AI catches requirements humans miss."

**"We need more credits for the price."**
- **Response:** "Our Pro plan gives you 300 credits - that's 3 PRDs per day. Most teams use 100-200/month. Would a custom plan work better?"

### Feature Objections

**"We need feature X that you don't have."**
- **Response:** "That's valuable feedback. We're constantly adding features based on customer needs. Can you tell me more about how you'd use feature X?"
- **Alternative:** "Our enterprise plan includes custom development. Would you be interested in discussing that?"

**"Your AI isn't as good as [competitor]."**
- **Response:** "We use state-of-the-art models and continuously improve. Can you show me an example where our AI fell short? We'll use it to improve."

**"We need better integrations."**
- **Response:** "We offer API access on all plans and are adding new integrations monthly. Which tools are most important to integrate with?"

### Process Objections

**"We're not ready to change our process."**
- **Response:** "PRDForge complements existing processes. You can start with one project and expand as you see value. Many teams use it alongside their current tools."

**"We need approval from [executive]."**
- **Response:** "I can prepare an executive summary and ROI analysis for [executive]. When would be a good time to schedule that discussion?"

**"We're in the middle of another project."**
- **Response:** "Perfect timing! You can try PRDForge with a small pilot project that won't disrupt your current work. It takes just 15 minutes to set up."

### Trial Objections

**"We want to try it free first."**
- **Response:** "Great! The free plan gives you 10 PRDs to test. I recommend trying it with a real project this week. I'll follow up in 3 days to help with any questions."

**"We need a longer trial."**
- **Response:** "Our free plan is unlimited time with 10 credits/month. You can use it as long as you need to evaluate. Most teams know within 2 weeks if it's right for them."

**"We want to test with our whole team."**
- **Response:** "The free plan supports 3 team members. For larger teams, we can set up a 14-day trial of the Pro plan. How many team members would need access?"

## Success Metrics

### Individual Rep Metrics
- **Quota Attainment:** % of quarterly quota
- **Deal Size:** Average contract value
- **Sales Cycle:** Days from lead to close
- **Win Rate:** % of opportunities won
- **Upsell Rate:** % of customers upgrading

### Team Metrics
- **Pipeline Growth:** Month-over-month increase
- **Conversion Rate:** Lead to opportunity to close
- **Customer Acquisition Cost:** Total sales cost / new customers
- **Revenue Per Rep:** Average revenue per salesperson
- **Team Attainment:** % of team hitting quota

### Quality Metrics
- **Customer Satisfaction:** Post-sale survey scores
- **Implementation Success:** % of successful onboardings
- **Referenceability:** % of customers willing to be references
- **Renewal Rate:** % of customers renewing
- **Expansion Revenue:** Revenue from existing customers
