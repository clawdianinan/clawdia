# VIS-001: Launch Marketing Visuals

## Brand Identity Summary
Based on analysis of the PRDForge logo:
- **Primary Color:** Royal Blue (#3F63FF) - Used for hammer icon and "forge" text
- **Secondary Color:** Dark Slate (#2F3A43) - Used for "prd" text and square outlines
- **Typography:** Geometric sans-serif (similar to Montserrat/Avenir) - All lowercase, medium weight
- **Style:** Modern, minimal, tech-oriented with industrial/craft metaphor
- **Aesthetic:** Friendly-professional balance, flat design, clean and scalable
