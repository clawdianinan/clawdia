# STRAT-003: Growth Forecasting Model

## Executive Summary
This growth forecasting model projects user acquisition, activation, retention, and revenue metrics for PRDForge's first 90 days post-launch. The model includes baseline projections, best-case/worst-case scenarios, and identifies key growth levers for optimization. Dashboard requirements are defined for real-time tracking and decision-making.

## Growth Framework

### Funnel Stages
1. **Acquisition:** Visitors → Signups
2. **Activation:** Signups → First PRD created
3. **Retention:** Active users → Returning users
4. **Revenue:** Active users → Paying customers
5. **Referral:** Paying customers → New referrals

### Key Growth Metrics
- **Acquisition Rate:** Signups / Visitors
- **Activation Rate:** Activated users / Signups  
- **Day 1 Retention:** Users active on Day 1 who return on Day 2
- **Day 7 Retention:** Users active on Day 1 who return on Day 7
- **Conversion Rate:** Paying customers / Activated users
- **Average Revenue Per User (ARPU):** Monthly revenue / Paying customers
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):** ARPU × Average customer lifespan
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** Marketing spend / New customers

## Growth Levers & Optimization Opportunities

### Acquisition Levers
1. **Channel Optimization**
   - **SEO:** Target "PRD template," "product requirements document"
   - **Content Marketing:** Blog posts on product management best practices
   - **Social Media:** LinkedIn targeting product managers, Twitter threads
   - **Paid Acquisition:** LinkedIn Ads, Google Ads (after validation)
   - **Partnerships:** Integration with product management tools

2. **Conversion Rate Optimization**
   - Landing page A/B testing (headlines, CTAs, social proof)
   - Signup flow simplification (reduce friction)
   - Value proposition clarity (benefits vs. features)
   - Trust signals (security badges, testimonials)

### Activation Levers
1. **Onboarding Optimization**
   - Guided first PRD creation (interactive tutorial)
   - Template library for quick starts
   - Progress tracking and completion rewards
   - Welcome email sequence with tips

2. **Product Experience**
   - Default project setup (reduce blank slate anxiety)
   - Quick wins (easy exports, sharing features)
   - Error prevention and helpful error messages
   - Performance optimization (fast load times)

### Retention Levers
1. **Engagement Features**
   - Notifications for project updates
   - Weekly usage reports and insights
   - New feature announcements
   - Community features (template sharing)

2. **Value Reinforcement**
   - Usage statistics and ROI demonstration
   - Success stories and case studies
   - Regular product updates and improvements
   - Customer feedback incorporation

### Revenue Levers
1. **Pricing Optimization**
   - Plan tier testing (features vs. price)
   - Annual discount incentives
   - Team/enterprise pricing
   - Usage-based pricing components

2. **Upsell/Cross-sell**
   - Feature highlighting for upgrade prompts
   - Team collaboration features
   - Advanced analytics and reporting
   - API access and integrations

### Referral Levers
1. **Viral Mechanisms**
   - Shareable PRD exports (with branding)
   - Collaborative editing (invite colleagues)
   - Referral rewards (discounts, credits)
   - Social sharing of templates

## Optimization Playbook

### Week 1-2: Foundation & Validation
**Focus:** Core metrics stabilization, initial learning
- **Day 1-3:** Monitor real-time metrics, fix critical issues
- **Day 4-7:** Analyze early patterns, identify optimization opportunities
- **Day 8-14:** Implement first optimization tests (landing page, onboarding)

### Week 3-4: Optimization & Scaling
**Focus:** Conversion rate improvement, channel testing
- **Week 3:** A/B test signup flow, optimize activation
- **Week 4:** Test initial paid channels, refine pricing
- **Key Tests:** Pricing page variants, email sequence timing, feature highlighting

### Month 2: Acceleration & Efficiency
**Focus:** Scale successful channels, improve retention
- **Week 5-6:** Double down on top-performing channels
- **Week 7-8:** Implement retention features, reduce churn
- **Key Initiatives:** Referral program launch, retention email series, feature updates

### Month 3: Sustainable Growth
**Focus:** LTV optimization, efficient scaling
- **Week 9-10:** Optimize pricing tiers, increase ARPU
- **Week 11-12:** Scale efficient channels, improve margins
- **Key Focus:** Customer lifetime value, referral acceleration, team expansion

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) by Phase

### Launch Phase (Days 1-14)
- **Primary KPI:** Activation Rate (target: 60%+)
- **Secondary KPIs:** Day 1 Retention (40%+), Support CSAT (90%+)
- **Leading Indicators:** Signup growth, server stability
- **Lagging Indicators:** Revenue, paying customers

### Growth Phase (Days 15-60)
- **Primary KPI:** Weekly Active Users growth (20%+ week-over-week)
- **Secondary KPIs:** Conversion Rate (10%+), Week 1 Retention (30%+)
- **Leading Indicators:** Channel efficiency, viral coefficient
- **Lagging Indicators:** MRR growth, LTV:CAC ratio

### Scaling Phase (Days 61-90)
- **Primary KPI:** Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) growth (30%+ month-over-month)
- **Secondary KPIs:** Customer Lifetime Value (LTV > $300), Net Revenue Retention (100%+)
- **Leading Indicators:** Referral rate, expansion revenue
- **Lagging Indicators:** Profit margin, cash flow
