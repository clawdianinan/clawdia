Executive Summary

IHS Towers has proposed adopting the IIH (Ilorin Innovation Hub) logo for their planned Maiduguri Innovation Hub. The attached "Consent Agreement" grants IHS unlimited, exclusive, perpetual rights to use, modify, license, and register IIH's intellectual property nationwide. This proposal presents significant legal and strategic risks to KWSG/IIH and requires immediate, careful negotiation.

1. Critical Legal Risks

🚨 IMMEDIATE CONCERNS

  • Excessive Rights: IHS seeks exclusive, perpetual rights to modify and register KWSG's IP
  • No Consideration: KWSG receives no benefits in return
