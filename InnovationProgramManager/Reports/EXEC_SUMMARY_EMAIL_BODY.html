Please find attached the consolidated draft report: “Innovation Program Manager – Global Research & Product Blueprint.”

Executive Summary:

  • We analyzed 12 global and regional innovation program models across accelerators, ecosystem hubs, and government-backed schemes.
  • The strongest programs combine structured cohort operations (selection, mentorship, milestones, reporting) with flexible delivery and funding architectures (equity, grants, hybrid).
  • Based on these patterns, we recommend IIH build a modular Innovation Program Manager (IPM) platform with five core blocks: Program Builder, Selection Engine, Delivery Engine, Budget/Funding Engine, and Impact Intelligence.
  • Recommended launch strategy is a grant + cohort hybrid MVP, with phased rollout over 90–180 days.

The report includes: methodology, global comparison tables, reusable program frameworks, budget modeling framework, product architecture, MVP roadmap, and appendices with source references.
