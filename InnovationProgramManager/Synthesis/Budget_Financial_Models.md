# Budget & Financial Models

## Comparative Financial Archetypes

## 1) Equity-Based Accelerator Model
**References:** YC, Techstars, 500 Global

### Revenue Logic
- Program operator invests in startup for equity/SAFE participation.
- Economic upside from portfolio winners over long horizon.

### Budget Implications
- High up-front founder support costs acceptable if portfolio thesis is strong.
- Need long-term portfolio tracking beyond cohort completion.

## 2) Equity-Free Grant Model
**References:** Start-Up Chile, parts of CcHUB/iHub ecosystem programs

### Revenue Logic
- Public/donor funding disbursed against milestones.
- Program value measured via ecosystem outcomes, not equity returns.

### Budget Implications
- Strong compliance overhead (documentation, audits, milestone proof).
- Cash flow tied to disbursement rules and reporting cycles.

## 3) Matching-Grant + Partner Delivery Model
**References:** Startup SG

### Revenue Logic
- Public contribution matched by founder/partner commitments.
- Delivery through accredited mentors/partners.

### Budget Implications
- Must model who pays what, when, and under what evidence conditions.
- Requires granular line-item eligibility rules.

## 4) Hybrid Model
**References:** Many African hubs + platform operators

### Revenue Logic
- Mix of grants, sponsorships, service partnerships, and occasional venture participation.

### Budget Implications
- Multi-source income planner required.
- Program-level and organization-level P&L both needed.

## Standard Cost Buckets (for IIH budget engine)
1. Personnel (program, ops, finance, mentor management)
2. Learning Delivery (speakers, facilitators, content)
3. Founder Support (stipends, travel, internet, tooling)
4. Events (kickoff, midpoint, demo day)
5. Partner/Platform (software, CRM, communication, legal)
6. Monitoring & Evaluation (data collection, reporting)
7. Contingency (5–12% based on risk profile)

## Budget Design Rules
- Separate **fixed** vs **variable** costs.
- Compute per-startup and per-cohort unit costs.
- Tie milestone disbursements to evidence artifacts.
- Include scenario toggles: conservative / expected / aggressive.

## Suggested Funding Mix Scenarios
- **Scenario A (Private accelerator):** equity + sponsorship + perks
- **Scenario B (Public cohort):** grant + matching contribution + service partners
- **Scenario C (IIH hybrid):** donor grant + partner support + fee-based advisory extension

## Minimum KPI Set for Financial Health
- Cost per startup served
- Program completion rate
- Follow-on capital raised (or grant success rate)
- Revenue sustainability ratio (non-grant recurring / total cost)
- Budget variance by category
