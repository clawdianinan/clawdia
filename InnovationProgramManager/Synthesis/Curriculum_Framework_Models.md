# Curriculum Framework Models

## Objective
Define reusable curriculum blueprints derived from analyzed global programs.

## Model 1: Investor-Readiness Accelerator (12 weeks)
**Reference pattern:** YC / Techstars / 500

### Weekly Arc
- **Weeks 1–2:** Diagnosis + traction baselining
- **Weeks 3–6:** Product/market validation sprints
- **Weeks 7–9:** Growth and GTM experiments
- **Weeks 10–11:** Fundraising narrative + data room prep
- **Week 12:** Demo day + investor warm intros

### Required Components
- Weekly office hours
- Biweekly expert sessions
- Founder peer accountability circles
- KPI review dashboard

## Model 2: Venture Studio / Builder (16 weeks)
**Reference pattern:** MEST-style education-to-build pipelines

### Phase Blocks
1. Problem discovery and user research
2. Solution prototyping and validation
3. Business model and go-to-market design
4. Pilot launch and early revenue tests

### Required Components
- Structured learning modules (technical + business)
- Capstone checkpoints
- Team formation / co-founder fit tools

## Model 3: Public Program / Grant Compliance (20–24 weeks)
**Reference pattern:** Start-Up Chile / Startup SG

### Phase Blocks
1. Eligibility onboarding + grant orientation
2. Milestone plan approval
3. Execution with evidence capture
4. Mid-term review and disbursement gate
5. Final reporting and impact evaluation

### Required Components
- Milestone ledger
- Compliance evidence uploads
- Disbursement workflow
- Audit trail

## Model 4: Ecosystem Platform (rolling)
**Reference pattern:** CcHUB / iHub / Station F platform modes

### Tracks (parallel)
- Founder support
- Corporate challenge track
- Community events/learning
- Partner referrals/services

### Required Components
- Rolling intake and matchmaking
- Segment-based content journeys
- CRM-style relationship tracking

## Cross-Model Core Curriculum Taxonomy
1. Founder & Team Development
2. Product & Customer Discovery
3. Business Model & Unit Economics
4. Growth Marketing & Sales
5. Legal, Governance, and Risk
6. Fundraising / Grants and Reporting
7. Leadership and Stakeholder Communication

## Curriculum-to-Data Mapping (for product)
- Module -> Session -> Deliverable -> Milestone -> Outcome metric
- Every module should map to at least one measurable founder/team behavior change or business KPI.

## Suggested IIH Default Curriculum (MVP)
- **Track A:** 12-week accelerator
- **Track B:** 20-week public/grant-driven program
- **Track C:** Rolling founder support light-touch lane
