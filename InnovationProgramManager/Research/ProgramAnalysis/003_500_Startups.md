# Program Analysis: 500 Startups

## 1. Program Name & Organization
- **Program Name:** 500 Startups Accelerator Program
- **Organization:** 500 Global (formerly 500 Startups)
- **Year Founded:** 2010
- **Headquarters:** San Francisco, California, USA

## 2. Geographic Focus & Reach
- **Primary Focus:** Global with regional specialization
- **Physical Locations:** San Francisco (headquarters) + regional funds worldwide
- **Regional Focus:** Multiple regional funds (Latin America, Middle East, Southeast Asia, etc.)
- **Global Reach:** 2,500+ companies funded across 75+ countries

## 3. Program Structure

### 3.1 Duration
- **Program Length:** 4 months (standard accelerator program)
- **Batch Frequency:** Multiple batches per year across different regions
- **Example Schedule:**
  - Batch applications open quarterly
  - Program runs in 4-month cycles
  - Demo Days at program conclusion

### 3.2 Format
- **Primary Format:** Hybrid (in-person and virtual components)
- **Core Components:**
  - 1-month remote preparation
  - 3-month intensive program (mix of in-person and virtual)
  - Demo Day preparation and execution
- **Location Options:**
  - San Francisco batches (in-person focus)
  - Regional batches (local in-person with virtual elements)
  - Virtual participation options available

### 3.3 Cohort Size
- **Batch Size:** 30-40 companies per batch
- **Group Structure:** Smaller working groups within cohort
- **Annual Capacity:** 150-200 companies across all programs
- **Diversity Focus:** Strong emphasis on diverse founders

## 4. Curriculum Framework

### 4.1 Learning Tracks
- **Structured Curriculum:** "Growth Framework" methodology
- **Focus Areas:**
  - Growth marketing and customer acquisition
  - Fundraising preparation and investor relations
  - Product development and metrics
  - Team building and leadership
- **Specialized Tracks:** Some batches focus on specific sectors (fintech, edtech, etc.)

### 4.2 Core Modules
- **Month 1 (Remote):** Preparation and baseline establishment
- **Months 2-3 (Intensive):** Core program execution
  - Weekly workshops and sessions
  - Mentor meetings and office hours
  - Growth experiments and metric tracking
- **Month 4:** Demo Day preparation and fundraising

### 4.3 Mentorship Structure
- **Mentor Network:** 5,000+ mentors worldwide
- **Mentorship Format:**
  - Group mentorship sessions
  - One-on-one office hours
  - Industry-specific mentor matching
- **Access:** Regular mentor interactions throughout program

### 4.4 Demo Day/Graduation Requirements
- **Demo Day:** Culminating investor pitch event
- **Preparation:** Intensive pitch coaching and practice
- **Outcome:** Investor introductions and fundraising support
- **Post-Program:** Continued investor access and alumni network

## 5. Financial Model

### 5.1 Funding Type
- **Primary Model:** Equity-based investment
- **Investment Structure:** $150,000 investment for equity stake
- **Additional Models:** Some regional funds may have variations

### 5.2 Typical Investment/Grants
- **Total Investment:** $150,000 per company
- **Equity Terms:** 6% equity for $150,000 investment
- **Additional Benefits:**
  - $250,000 in partner credits and discounts
  - Access to follow-on funding from 500 Global network

### 5.3 Program Costs (if disclosed)
- **Cost to Startups:** No program fees
- **All Costs Embedded:** Program costs covered by equity taken
- **Travel/Living:** Companies responsible for own expenses
- **Software Perks:** Significant partner discounts and credits
