# Program Analysis: Techstars

## 1. Program Name & Organization
- **Program Name:** Techstars Accelerator Program
- **Organization:** Techstars
- **Year Founded:** 2006
- **Headquarters:** Boulder, Colorado, USA

## 2. Geographic Focus & Reach
- **Primary Focus:** Global network with local programs
- **Physical Locations:** 30+ cities worldwide (Boston, London, Tel Aviv, etc.)
- **Regional Focus:** City-specific programs with global network access
- **Global Reach:** 3,200+ companies funded across 30+ countries

## 3. Program Structure

### 3.1 Duration
- **Program Length:** 3 months (standard accelerator)
- **Additional Programs:**
  - Founder Catalyst: 10-week virtual pre-accelerator for early-stage founders
  - Various specialized programs (sector-specific, corporate partnerships)
- **Batch Frequency:** Applications open twice yearly for each program
- **Monthly Terms:** Since 2023, supporting 600+ founders yearly

### 3.2 Format
- **Primary Format:** Hybrid (in-person, hybrid, and remote options)
- **Program Options:**
  - Techstars Anywhere: Limited in-person meets with remote focus
  - City-specific programs: In-person with local ecosystem integration
- **Core Components:**
  - Month 1: "Mentor Madness" and customer development
  - Month 2: Product development and workshops
  - Month 3: Pitch preparation and Demo Day
- **Location Flexibility:** Multiple format options to accommodate different needs

### 3.3 Cohort Size
- **Standard Cohort:** Approximately 12 companies per program
- **Annual Capacity:** 600+ founders across all programs
- **Selection Funnel:**
  - Thousands of applications → Top 100 interviews → Top 50 second round
  - Top 30 deep evaluation → ~24 feedback → ~12 final selections
- **Acceptance Rate:** 3-4% (highly competitive)

## 4. Curriculum Framework

### 4.1 Learning Tracks
- **Structured Phases:** Three distinct monthly phases
- **Phase 1 (Month 1):** Exploration and "Mentor Madness"
  - Focus: Customer development and intensive mentor matching
  - Activities: Mentor meetings, customer discovery, market validation
- **Phase 2 (Month 2):** Product development and traction
  - Focus: Building, gaining traction, workshops
  - Activities: Masterclasses on fundraising, marketing, hiring
- **Phase 3 (Month 3):** Pitch preparation and Demo Day
  - Focus: Pitch honing and investor networking
  - Activities: Practice sessions, Demo Day preparation

### 4.2 Core Modules
- **Mentorship-Driven:** Emphasis on mentor relationships over formal curriculum
- **Workshop Topics:**
  - Fundraising strategies and investor relations
  - Marketing and customer acquisition
  - Hiring and team building
  - Product development and scaling
  - Legal and operational best practices
- **Masterclasses:** Expert-led sessions on specific topics

### 4.3 Mentorship Structure
- **Mentor Network:** 10,000+ mentors globally
- **Mentorship Format:**
  - "Mentor Madness": Intensive mentor matching in first month
  - Ongoing mentor relationships throughout program
  - Alumni mentorship and peer support
- **Access:** Extensive network of experts, investors, and alumni

### 4.4 Demo Day/Graduation Requirements
- **Demo Day:** Culminating 6-minute pitch event to investors
- **Preparation:** Extended practice compared to other accelerators
- **Outcome:** Investor introductions and networking opportunities
- **Post-Program:** Continued access to network and resources

## 5. Financial Model

### 5.1 Funding Type
- **Primary Model:** Equity-based investment
- **Investment Structure:** SAFE agreements for common stock
- **Equity Terms:** $20,000 for 6% equity (legacy standard)
- **Updated Terms (Fall 2025):** $220,000 total investment
  - $20,000 for 5% equity
  - Optional $200,000 SAFE note (20% discount, $3-5M valuation cap, or MFN clause)

### 5.2 Typical Investment/Grants
- **Total Investment:** Up to $220,000 per startup
- **Breakdown:**
  - $20,000 for 6% equity (standard deal)
  - Optional additional $100,000 convertible note for 3% more equity (legacy)
  - Updated: $20,000 for 5% + optional $200,000 SAFE
- **Additional Benefits:** $2-4M+ in perks and credits
- **No Program Fees:** Explicitly no monetary cost to join

### 5.3 Program Costs (if disclosed)
- **Cost to Startups:** $0 in program fees
- **All Costs Embedded:** Program costs covered by equity taken
- **Operational Support:** Corporate partners pay ~$50,000 annual fees for back-office operations
- **Software Perks:** $250,000+ in DigitalOcean credits, AWS/Google credits, legal/hosting services

## 6. Selection Process

### 6.1 Application Criteria
- **Open to:** Startups at various stages (no strict revenue requirements)
- **Key Evaluation Factors (ranked by importance):**
  1. **Team (emphasized 3x):** Grit, coachability, complementary skills, adaptability, teamwork
  2. **Market Potential:** Real market opportunity, understanding of market size and competition
  3. **Traction:** Tangible progress (prototype, user tests, pre-orders, pilot customers)
  4. **Core Idea:** Strength of fundamental business concept
- **Team Dynamics:** History of working together, complementary skills emphasized

### 6.2 Selection Timeline
- **Application Process:** 1-4 hours to complete initially
- **Review Timeline:** 1-4 weeks after deadline for interview invitations
- **Interview Stages:**
  1. Initial screening by managing directors
  2. Top 100 → first-round interviews
  3. Top 50 → second-round interviews
  4. Top 30 → deep evaluation
  5. ~24 → additional feedback
  6. ~12 → final offers
- **Final Decision:** Within 8 weeks of application closing

### 6.3 Success Rates
- **Acceptance Rate:** 3-4% (highly competitive)
- **Reapplication Success:** Many successful Techstars companies joined after multiple attempts
- **Screening Committee Chance:** ~50% chance if reaching screening committee
- **Encouragement:** Explicitly encourages reapplication
