# Program Analysis: MEST Africa

## 1. Program Name & Organization
- **Program Name:** MEST Entrepreneurial Training Program / MEST portfolio support
- **Organization:** Meltwater Entrepreneurial School of Technology (MEST)
- **Year Founded:** 2008
- **Headquarters:** Accra, Ghana

## 2. Geographic Focus & Reach
- **Primary Focus:** Africa (notably Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa, Côte d'Ivoire)
- **Physical Location:** Accra core operations with pan-African programming
- **Global Reach:** Alumni/portfolio with international market expansion

## 3. Program Structure
- Flagship training historically 12-month full-time format (program variants exist)
- Post-training incubation/investment pathway for selected teams
- Cohort-based founder development

## 4. Curriculum Framework
- Software/product development, business fundamentals, venture building, pitch preparation
- Strong experiential training + mentorship

## 5. Financial Model
- Sponsored training model + seed investment into selected ventures
- Equity participation through portfolio investments

## 6. Selection Process
- Competitive application, assessments/interviews, final cohort selection
- Team quality, technical capability, market opportunity emphasized

## 7. Success Metrics
- Publicly referenced: 90+ startups and $30M+ invested over time (subject to current updates)
- Strong regional founder pipeline impact

## 8. Operational Insights
- Talent-to-venture pipeline design (training -> incubation -> capital)
- Pan-African recruiting with localized support structures

## 9. Publicly Available Templates
- Program application pages and eligibility guidance available publicly
