# Program Analysis: Startup SG

## 1. Program Name & Organization
- **Program Name:** Startup SG (with Startup SG Founder as key founder-entry program)
- **Organization:** Enterprise Singapore
- **Year Founded:** Startup SG umbrella launched 2017 (Founder scheme evolved over time)
- **Headquarters:** Singapore

## 2. Geographic Focus & Reach
- **Primary Focus:** Singapore-based startup ecosystem development
- **Regional Focus:** Singapore with global market orientation for supported startups

## 3. Program Structure
- Umbrella of multiple schemes (Founder, Tech, Equity, Talent, etc.)
- Founder track delivered via accredited mentor partners (AMPs)

## 4. Curriculum Framework
- Mentorship-led founder development, commercialization support, milestone tracking

## 5. Financial Model
- **Primary Model:** Matching grants in founder-stage programs + co-investment mechanisms in other tracks
- **Founder Track:** Publicly documented matching support (terms updated periodically)

## 6. Selection Process
- Apply through AMPs; evaluation based on innovation, feasibility, team capability, market potential

## 7. Success Metrics
- Singapore startup ecosystem strength and policy continuity are major indicators
- Program-level metrics distributed across Enterprise Singapore reporting channels

## 8. Operational Insights
- Distributed delivery through accredited partners with central policy governance
- Strong compliance and milestone-driven public funding controls

## 9. Publicly Available Templates
- Public eligibility guides, partner lists, application pathways

## 10. Research Sources & Citations
### Primary Sources:

## 11. Key Insights & Patterns
1. Mentor-partner delivery model improves scalability of government programs
2. Matching-grant structure aligns founder commitment with public support
3. Strong institutional design for accountability and continuity
