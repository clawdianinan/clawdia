# Program Analysis: Startup Chile

## 1. Program Name & Organization
- **Program Name:** Start-Up Chile (Build / Ignite / Growth tracks)
- **Organization:** CORFO (Government of Chile)
- **Year Founded:** 2010
- **Headquarters:** Santiago, Chile

## 2. Geographic Focus & Reach
- **Primary Focus:** Chile with global founder attraction
- **Regional Focus:** Latin America + international startups relocating/operating in Chile

## 3. Program Structure
- Track-based public program for different startup maturity levels
- Durations vary by track; support windows often extend through milestone periods

## 4. Curriculum Framework
- Acceleration, mentorship, market validation, growth/internationalization support

## 5. Financial Model
- **Primary Model:** Equity-free public grants (track-specific)
- **Grant Structure:** Publicly disclosed CLP-denominated grants by track
- **Co-financing:** Required co-investment/matching depending on track rules

## 6. Selection Process
- Periodic calls, eligibility checks, panel evaluation, milestone-based disbursement

## 7. Success Metrics
- Globally recognized public accelerator benchmark with strong international alumni visibility

## 8. Operational Insights
