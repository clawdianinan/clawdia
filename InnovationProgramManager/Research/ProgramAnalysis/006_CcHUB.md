# Program Analysis: CcHUB

## 1. Program Name & Organization
- **Program Name:** CcHUB Innovation/Acceleration Programs
- **Organization:** Co-Creation Hub (CcHUB)
- **Year Founded:** 2010
- **Headquarters:** Lagos, Nigeria (with pan-African operations)

## 2. Geographic Focus & Reach
- **Primary Focus:** Nigeria and Africa
- **Physical Location:** Lagos + additional African nodes/program operations
- **Regional Focus:** West/East/Southern Africa program activity
- **Global Reach:** Pan-African partnerships and donor/corporate collaborations

## 3. Program Structure
- Multi-program model (fellowships, accelerators, venture support)
- Duration varies by program (e.g., 6-18 month windows in some public calls)
- Hybrid delivery across in-person and virtual components

## 4. Curriculum Framework
- Human-centered design, product development, go-to-market, impact measurement
- Strong applied mentorship and ecosystem linkage

## 5. Financial Model
- Mix of equity-free grants/challenge funding and venture support depending on program
- Frequent donor/corporate-backed cohorts (e.g., education/impact programs)

## 6. Selection Process
- Program-specific open calls
- Evaluation commonly covers impact, team capability, solution quality, scalability

## 7. Success Metrics
- Public reporting indicates extensive startup support and ecosystem-building outcomes
- Not all programs publish standardized acceptance/survival rates

## 8. Operational Insights
- Platform model with thematic verticals (education, health, governance, climate, etc.)
- Partner-heavy execution with foundations, corporates, and development agencies

## 9. Publicly Available Templates
- Application calls and eligibility criteria publicly posted per cohort
