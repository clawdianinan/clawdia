### 2.2 Specific Legal Risks

#### A. Intellectual Property Erosion
- **Risk:** IHS could register trademarks for modified versions
- **Impact:** KWSG could lose control over own brand
- **Precedent:** Sets dangerous precedent for other IP

#### B. Brand Dilution & Confusion
- **Risk:** Multiple "Innovation Hubs" with similar branding
- **Impact:** Confusion in ecosystem, diluted IIH brand value
- **Example:** "IHS Innovation Hub Maiduguri" vs "Ilorin Innovation Hub"

#### C. Unlimited Geographic Expansion
- **Risk:** IHS can use logo for hubs anywhere in Nigeria
- **Impact:** No control over quality, alignment with KWSG objectives
- **Strategic:** Could compete with KWSG initiatives

#### D. Perpetual, Irrevocable Rights
- **Risk:** Cannot terminate even if relationship sours
- **Impact:** Permanent loss of control over brand assets
- **Financial:** No ongoing revenue or benefits
