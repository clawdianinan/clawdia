## 2. Independence Impact Assessment

### 2.1 Direct Constraints
1. **Geographic Exclusivity**: If IHS uses IIH logo nationwide, IIH cannot:
   - Expand to other states with same branding
   - Partner with other telcos/infrastructure providers
   - Establish independent innovation hubs elsewhere

2. **Brand Identity Control**: IIH would lose control over:
   - How logo is modified/adapted by IHS
   - Quality standards for logo usage
   - Association with IHS business activities

3. **Future Partnership Limitations**:
   - Other potential partners (MTN, Airtel, Glo) may see IIH as "IHS-aligned"
   - Government partnerships in other states may conflict with IHS presence
   - International development partners may prefer neutral branding

### 2.2 Long-term Strategic Implications

**5-Year Outlook if Proposal Accepted:**
- IIH becomes nationally recognized as "IHS Innovation Hub network"
- All expansion requires IHS partnership or approval
- Brand equity accrues primarily to IHS, not IIH/KWSG
- Limited ability to pivot or rebrand as market evolves

**5-Year Outlook with Protective Agreement:**
- IIH maintains independent brand identity
- Can expand selectively with various partners
- Controls quality and usage of its IP
- Preserves future strategic options

## 3. Strategic Alternatives Analysis

- **Limited geographic scope** (Maiduguri only, not nationwide)
- **Non-exclusive license** (IIH retains all rights)
- **Fixed term** (3-5 years, not perpetual)

- **Attribution requirement** ("In partnership with Ilorin Innovation Hub")

### 3.3 Option C: Strategic Partnership Framework
- Formal innovation partnership beyond just logo usage
- IIH provides innovation hub design/operations expertise
- Revenue sharing from IHS innovation hubs
