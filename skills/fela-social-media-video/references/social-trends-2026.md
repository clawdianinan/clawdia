# Social Media Trends 2026

## Platform-Specific Trends

### Instagram Reels
- **Audio trends**: Original sounds > licensed music (algorithm preference)
- **Text-on-screen**: Captions mandatory (80% watch without sound)
- **Series content**: Episode recaps + behind-the-scenes
- **Interactive elements**: Polls, quizzes, "choose your ending"
- **Vertical series**: Multi-part stories across Reels

### TikTok
- **Duet culture**: Reaction videos to popular content
- **Green screen**: Educational content with visual aids
- **Storytelling**: 3-part mini-series within 60 seconds
- **Sound trends**: Remixes, voiceovers, ambient sounds
- **Hashtag challenges**: Branded challenges for fan engagement

### YouTube Shorts
- **Clip-to-full**: Teaser clips driving to full episodes
- **Creator collaboration**: Cross-channel Shorts
- **Music integration**: YouTube Audio Library trending tracks
- **Community tab**: Shorts promoting polls/discussions
- **Shopping integration**: Product tags in Shorts

## Content Formats Performing Well

### Educational Entertainment ("Edutainment")
- Historical events explained through animation
- Cultural insights with humor
- "What if" scenarios exploring alternative history
- Character-driven lessons (ape protagonists teaching history)

### Behind-the-Scenes
- Voice actor recordings
- Animation process (sketch → final)
- Script writing sessions
- Sound design/music composition

### Fan Engagement
- Character Q&A (voice actors responding as characters)
- "Which ape are you?" personality quizzes
- Fan art showcases
- Episode discussion prompts

## Algorithm Insights

### Instagram
- **Completion rate**: >70% gets maximum reach
- **Shares**: Most valuable engagement metric
- **Saves**: Indicates "reference value" content
- **Time of day**: 7-9 PM local time peak engagement

### TikTok
- **Watch time**: First 3 seconds critical
- **Re-watches**: High re-watch rate boosts visibility
- **Comment engagement**: Replies to comments extend reach
- **Follow-through**: Profile visits after video

### YouTube Shorts
- **Swipe-up rate**: Percentage watching next Short
- **Subscribe rate**: From Shorts to channel subscription
- **Cross-promotion**: Shorts driving to long-form views

## AOZ Optimization Strategies

### Series Launch
1. **Pre-launch**: Character introductions, world-building teasers
2. **Launch day**: Episode clips, creator commentary, fan reactions
3. **Post-launch**: Behind-the-scenes, voice actor features, Easter eggs

### Ongoing Engagement
- **Weekly**: 2-3 Shorts/Reels per episode
- **Monthly**: Fan spotlight, Q&A session, progress updates
- **Quarterly**: Season recap, upcoming teasers, milestone celebration

### Cross-Platform Strategy
- **Instagram**: Visual storytelling, aesthetic consistency
- **TikTok**: Trend participation, duet challenges
- **YouTube**: Episode extensions, deep dives
- **Twitter**: Episode discussions, fan interactions
