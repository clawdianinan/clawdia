# Social Media Video Skill

## Platform Specifications

### Instagram Reels
- **Duration**: 15-90 seconds (optimal: 30-45s)
- **Aspect ratio**: 9:16 (1080x1920)
- **Hook time**: First 3 seconds must capture attention
- **Text overlay**: Large, bold, minimal words
- **Audio**: Trending sounds or original score with caption
- **CTA**: "Watch full episode on YouTube" + link in bio

### TikTok
- **Duration**: 15-60 seconds (optimal: 21-34s)
- **Hook**: Immediate value or curiosity gap
- **Trend participation**: Use trending sounds/hashtags when relevant
- **Pacing**: Fast cuts (every 2-3 seconds)
- **Caption**: Question or call to comment
- **Duets/Stitches**: Plan for reaction content

### YouTube Shorts
- **Duration**: ≤60 seconds
- **Vertical format**: 9:16
- **Source material**: Clip from full episode with added text/effects
- **CTA**: "Full episode in description" + pinned comment with link
- **Series branding**: AOZ logo watermark bottom-right

## Content Strategy

### Episode Teasers
1. **Cold open clip**: Most dramatic 30 seconds from episode
2. **Text overlay**: "Episode 1 out now → link in bio"
3. **End screen**: YouTube thumbnail + subscribe prompt

### Behind-the-Scenes
1. **Character design process**: Speedpaint + voiceover
2. **Voice actor clips**: Recording booth moments
3. **Script to screen**: Side-by-side comparison

### Viral Hooks
- **"You won't believe what happens next"**: Cliffhanger moment
- **"History retold"**: Historical scene with modern commentary
- **"Character reveal"**: New ape design with personality traits

## Production Workflow

### 1. Clip Selection
- Identify 2-3 key moments per episode (emotional peak, plot twist, humor)
- Extract 15-45 second clips maintaining narrative coherence
- Add captions for silent viewing

### 2. Platform Optimization
- **Reels**: Add trending audio, use Instagram-native text styles
- **TikTok**: Add green screen effects, use duet format for reactions
- **Shorts**: Use YouTube end screen elements, link to full video

### 3. Posting Schedule
- **Episode launch day**: 3 teasers (24h before, launch hour, 6h after)
- **Weekly engagement**: 2 BTS clips, 1 fan reaction/duet
- **Cross-posting**: Reels → TikTok → Shorts (with platform-specific tweaks)

## Technical Specifications

### Video Format
- **Resolution**: 1080x1920 (9:16)
- **Frame rate**: 30fps or 60fps for smooth motion
- **Bitrate**: 8-12 Mbps for quality
- **File format**: MP4 with H.264 codec

### Text & Graphics
- **Font**: Bold sans-serif (Impact, Arial Black, Montserrat Black)
- **Text size**: ≥5% of screen height
- **Contrast**: White text with black outline or vice versa
- **Position**: Safe area (avoid notch/camera cutout)

### Audio
- **Volume normalization**: -14 LUFS for consistent loudness
- **Music**: Copyright-free or licensed tracks
- **Voiceover**: Clear, energetic, pace-matched to visuals

## AOZ-Specific Rules

### Brand Consistency
- **Color palette**: Orange (#FF6B35), Black (#000000), Gold (#FFD700)
- **Logo placement**: Bottom-right, 10% opacity
- **Episode reference**: Always include "AOZ S01E01" in caption

### Content Guidelines
- **Tone**: Satirical but respectful of historical themes
- **Humor**: Witty, not crude; cultural, not offensive
- **Educational value**: Each clip should teach something about African history/culture
