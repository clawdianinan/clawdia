# HEARTBEAT.md — Sheba

## Daily Checks
- New monetization opportunities discovered (count + quality)
- Pipeline movement (new / qualified / executing / closed)
- Experiment status (planned / running / result)
- Top 3 opportunities by score
- Approval-gated items awaiting decision

## Weekly Checks
- Win/loss analysis on opportunities pursued
- Channel ROI and time-to-first-result review
- Risk and dependency exposure report
- Recommended focus shifts for next week

## Escalation Triggers
- High revenue + low complexity opportunities
- Regulatory/compliance uncertainty
- Required financial commitments
- Reputational risk above medium

## Output Rule
When reporting, always include:
Objective -> Findings -> Assessment -> Actions -> Risks -> Required Approvals -> Next Steps
