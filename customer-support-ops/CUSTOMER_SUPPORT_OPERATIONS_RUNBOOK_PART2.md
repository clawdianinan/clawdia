## 8. RISK MANAGEMENT

### 8.1 Identified Risks

#### High Priority Risks:
1. **Insufficient Team Capacity:** Support volume exceeds team capacity
   - **Mitigation:** Scalable hiring plan, temporary contractor network
   - **Monitoring:** Daily volume vs. capacity analysis

2. **Critical System Outages:** Major product issues overwhelming support
   - **Mitigation:** Incident response plan, executive escalation path
   - **Monitoring:** System health monitoring, early warning indicators

3. **Knowledge Gaps:** Team lacks expertise for complex issues
   - **Mitigation:** Comprehensive training program, expert escalation paths
   - **Monitoring:** Escalation rate analysis, quality assurance scores

4. **Customer Satisfaction Crisis:** Widespread customer dissatisfaction
   - **Mitigation:** Proactive communication, compensation policies
   - **Monitoring:** CSAT trends, social media sentiment

#### Medium Priority Risks:
5. **Tool Limitations:** Support systems insufficient for volume/complexity
   - **Mitigation:** Regular tool evaluation, scalable architecture
   - **Monitoring:** System performance metrics, team feedback

6. **Process Inefficiencies:** Bottlenecks in support workflow
   - **Mitigation:** Continuous improvement process, regular workflow audits
   - **Monitoring:** Process cycle times, team feedback

7. **Team Burnout:** High stress leading to turnover
   - **Mitigation:** Sustainable workload, recognition programs, career paths
   - **Monitoring:** Team satisfaction surveys, turnover rates

### 8.2 Risk Monitoring & Response

#### Daily Risk Monitoring:
- Review high-priority risk indicators
- Check system capacity vs. demand
- Monitor team stress levels
- Track critical incident status

#### Weekly Risk Review:
- Update risk assessment based on weekly performance
- Adjust mitigation strategies as needed
- Report risk status to management
- Plan risk reduction activities

#### Monthly Risk Assessment:
- Comprehensive risk review
- Update risk register
- Evaluate mitigation effectiveness
- Plan strategic risk reduction
