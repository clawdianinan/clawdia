## What is the Ilorin Innovation Hub (IIH)?

The **Ilorin Innovation Hub (IIH)**, also known as **Ilorin Tech Park** (legal entity: **Ilorin Tech Park Ltd**), is a technology and innovation hub located in Ilorin, Kwara State, Nigeria. It is the primary tech hub of the state, operating as a coworking space, startup incubator, skills training center, and community of innovators.

IIH is affiliated with **AfriLabs**, the pan-African network of science and technology hubs, and is embedded within the **IHS Towers** facility in Ilorin — a partnership formalized through a Collaboration Agreement with the Kwara State Government (KWSG).

**Core Mission**: To build a thriving innovation ecosystem in Kwara State and North-Central Nigeria by providing access to workspace, training, mentorship, programs, and partnerships for tech entrepreneurs, startups, and young professionals.

## Key Programs & Initiatives

### ORBIT (Internship Program)
Ilorin Innovation Hub's flagship internship and talent development program. Participants are onboarded into streams (Software Engineering, Graphic Design, Social Media, Telecoms, etc.) and matched with projects. Managed through a custom platform (`ilorin.tech` / ORBIT).

### Founders Match
A structured matchmaking event connecting startup founders with investors, co-founders, and mentors. Maiden edition held March 15, 2025 (`Founders Match 1.0`). Has a dedicated brand guide and event documentation.

### Ilorin Startup / Ilorin Startup Growth Program
Accelerator and growth program for early-stage startups in Ilorin. Includes training, mentorship, and access to resources.

### DevBox
Technical training workshop series. Notable sessions: "Building APIs & Microservices" (Introduction and Advanced), and "Getting Started with Prompt Engineering."

### E-Government Hackathon (January 2023)
A two-day hackathon focused on e-government solutions. Organized in collaboration with state government technology initiatives.

### Kwara AI Summit (2025)
A high-profile AI summit for Kwara State, featuring government, private sector, and development partners. IIH was a key organizer.

### Art Hackathon (EWA Hackathon)
A creative tech event combining art and technology. Winners received prize money and award letters from vendors (Atmos, CSI Media, Decor Nigeria, Grid Crux, Space Finish).

### Tech Skills Program / AI Bootcamp
Free and subsidized training programs on web development, responsive design, and AI. Linked to freeCodeCamp curriculum and original content.

### Webinar Series
Online learning events. Webinar 1 featured Helen Anatogu and focused on startup growth and innovation hubs.

### IIH Program Kick-Off (February 27, 2025)
Formal launch event for IIH's 2025 programming year. Invited AfriLabs and commissioners. Marked the beginning of IIH's formal operational phase at the IHS facility.

## Deduplication Policy for OpenClaw Agent

When integrating this archive into your local knowledge base:

1. **Check by content hash** (MD5 or SHA-256), not by filename alone. The same document may appear under different names in your existing files.
2. If a file in this archive is an **exact content match** to a file already in your local store, delete the archive copy to avoid duplication.
3. **Do not delete** archive copies that represent a specific version in a progression (e.g., a 2023 budget draft), even if a newer version exists in your local files — the older versions carry historical context.
4. For **logos and branding assets**, keep the archive copies if your local version is lower resolution or a different variant.
