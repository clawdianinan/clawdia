### KPI UPDATES

#### Incubation Program
- **Incubation conversion rate** does not apply (fixed number of startups expected)
- Frequency is annual, so monthly tracking cadence can't work
- **Delivery adherence** is important metric to measure here

#### Milestone Tracking
- **Milestone effectiveness** can't be tracked quarterly (incubation program runs 3 months)
- Program timeline: Roadshow (6 weeks) → Call for applications (6 weeks) → Judging (3 weeks) → Opening (1 week) → Incubation (12 weeks)
- **Pre-incubation and incubation** run concurrently
- **Program completion ratio** should apply to both pre-incubation and incubation

#### Ecosystem Metrics
- Track **number of ecosystem events** (required to deliver at least 40 events)
- Track **gender participation** (important metric for sponsoring partners)
- Track **geographical participation** (hub is designed to be regional)

#### Partnership Metrics
- **Partner retention** - Need to define what active partner role looks like
- Goal for year: Establish satellite innovation network leveraging partnerships developed across states visited last year

#### Acceleration Program
- Track **impact areas adherence** - Do startups align with corporate objectives?
- **Corporate innovation** - Important accelerator to pilot ratio
- **Gender participation**
- **Program completion rate**
- **Pilot to commercialisation ratio**

#### Technical Training
- **Learners attrition rate** on monthly basis is important
- **Gender participation** is also very important
- **Milestone adherence** is also very important
- **Can't track placement** for another 18 months when training is over

#### L2E Program
- Uses private LMS - can't track their scores
- Not important for us to track their scores

#### Cost Tracking
- **Can't track cost per participants** - fees are external to us and cover range of services not directly participant-led

#### Financial Oversight
- Finance does **not** have oversight on Cchub or FA's program finances
- Being negotiated and paid directly to them by IHS
- Their scope of work is what we manage, not the finances
