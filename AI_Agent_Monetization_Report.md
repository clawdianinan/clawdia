## 5. Risk Assessment & Mitigation

### Technical Risks:
1. **API Reliability** - Use multiple providers, implement fallbacks
2. **System Downtime** - Implement monitoring, backup systems
3. **Security Breaches** - Regular security audits, encryption

### Business Risks:
1. **Payment Delays** - Upfront payments, automated reminders
2. **Client Acquisition** - Leverage IIH network, referrals
3. **Regulatory Changes** - Stay informed, legal consultation

### Financial Risks:
1. **Currency Fluctuations** - Multi-currency accounts, hedging
2. **Tax Compliance** - Automated tracking, professional advice
3. **Cash Flow** - Maintain reserves, diversify income streams
