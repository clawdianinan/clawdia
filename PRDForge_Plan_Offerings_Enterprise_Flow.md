# PRDForge - Comprehensive Plan Offerings & Enterprise Flow

## Executive Summary

PRDForge is a product documentation platform that helps teams create, manage, and collaborate on product requirements documents (PRDs). This document outlines realistic, implementable pricing plans and enterprise workflows designed to drive sustainable growth while meeting the needs of organizations from solo founders to large enterprises.

---

## 1. COMPREHENSIVE PLAN OFFERINGS

### 1.1 Free Plan ($0/month) - "Explorer"
**Target:** Solo founders, students, early-stage startups
**Goal:** Drive product adoption and create upgrade incentives

**Limitations (Designed to Drive Upgrades):**
- **Projects:** 1 active project limit
- **Documents:** 3 PRD templates maximum
- **Collaborators:** 1 user only (no team sharing)
- **Storage:** 100MB total storage
- **Export Formats:** PDF only (no Word/Google Docs export)
- **Version History:** 30-day retention only
- **AI Features:** Basic suggestions only (no advanced AI generation)
- **Support:** Community forum only (no priority support)
- **Branding:** "Made with PRDForge" watermark on exports
- **API Access:** None
- **Custom Templates:** Not available

**Upgrade Triggers:**
- User hits project limit when starting second product
- Needs to collaborate with team members
- Requires professional export formats
- Needs more storage for attachments

### 1.2 Starter Plan ($12/month or $120/year) - "Builder"
**Target:** Small teams (2-10 people), early-stage startups
**Goal:** Clear value proposition for growing teams

**Core Features:**
- **Projects:** 5 active projects
- **Documents:** Unlimited PRD templates
- **Collaborators:** Up to 5 team members
- **Storage:** 5GB total storage
- **Export Formats:** PDF, Word, Markdown
- **Version History:** 1-year retention
- **AI Features:** Smart suggestions + basic generation
- **Support:** Email support (48-hour response)
- **Branding:** No watermarks
- **API Access:** Read-only API
- **Custom Templates:** 3 custom templates
- **Integrations:** Basic Slack/Teams notifications

**Value Proposition:**
"Everything a small team needs to document products professionally. Collaborate with your team, export to any format, and keep your documentation organized."

### 1.3 Pro Plan ($24/month or $240/year) - "Professional"
**Target:** Serious product teams, scaling startups, agencies
**Goal:** Premium features for power users

**Premium Features (Includes all Starter features plus):**
- **Projects:** Unlimited active projects
- **Collaborators:** Up to 20 team members
- **Storage:** 50GB total storage
- **Export Formats:** All formats + Google Docs sync
- **Version History:** Unlimited retention
- **AI Features:** Advanced AI generation + analysis
- **Support:** Priority email support (24-hour response)
- **API Access:** Full read/write API
- **Custom Templates:** Unlimited custom templates
- **Integrations:** Full Slack/Teams + Jira/GitHub integration
- **Advanced Features:**
  - Approval workflows
  - Custom branding (logo, colors)
  - Advanced analytics and reporting
  - Template marketplace access
  - Webhook support
  - SSO (SAML) ready

**Value Proposition:**
"Professional product documentation with enterprise-grade features. Scale your documentation process across multiple teams and products."

### 1.4 Enterprise Plan (Custom Pricing) - "Enterprise"
**Target:** Large organizations, enterprises, government
**Goal:** Full-featured with enterprise requirements

**Enterprise-Specific Features:**
- **Custom Pricing:** Volume-based or per-seat (typically $45-75/user/month)
- **Unlimited Everything:** Projects, collaborators, storage
- **Dedicated Infrastructure:** Isolated instance or VPC deployment
- **Security & Compliance:**
  - SOC 2 Type II compliance
  - GDPR/CCPA ready
  - HIPAA compliance available
  - Custom data retention policies
- **Advanced Admin Controls:**
  - Custom user roles and permissions
  - Audit logs and compliance reporting
  - Advanced SSO (SAML, OIDC, SCIM)
  - IP whitelisting
- **Support & Services:**
  - Dedicated account manager
  - 24/7 phone support
  - Custom onboarding and training
  - Quarterly business reviews
- **Custom Development:**
  - Custom integrations
  - White-label options
  - Custom feature development (minimum commitment)
- **Legal & Contract:**
  - Custom contracts
  - Data processing agreements
  - Security assessments

## 2. ENTERPRISE FLOW DESIGN

### 2.1 Realistic Enterprise Sales Process

**Phase 1: Discovery (1-2 weeks)**
- Initial contact through website form or sales outreach
- Qualification call (30 mins):
  - Understand team size and use case
  - Identify pain points with current documentation
  - Determine decision-makers and budget
- Send tailored proposal with 3 options

**Phase 2: Evaluation (2-4 weeks)**
- Technical deep dive (60 mins):
  - Demo tailored to enterprise needs
  - Security/compliance discussion
  - Integration requirements review
- Pilot program setup (optional):
  - 30-day free trial with 5 seats
  - Dedicated support during pilot
- Reference calls with similar customers

**Phase 3: Negotiation (1-2 weeks)**
- Custom pricing proposal based on:
  - Number of seats (volume discounts)
  - Contract length (annual vs. monthly)
  - Custom development requirements
- Legal review of contracts
- Security questionnaire completion

**Phase 4: Implementation (2-4 weeks)**
- Kickoff meeting with implementation team
- SSO and infrastructure setup
- Data migration planning
- User onboarding and training

### 2.2 Custom Pricing Negotiation Workflow

```
Enterprise Inquiry → Qualification → Needs Assessment → Proposal Generation
       ↓                    ↓               ↓                  ↓
   Web Form          Sales Call       Technical         Custom Quote
                                           ↓                  ↓
                                    Requirements       Negotiation Phase
                                           ↓                  ↓
                                    Security Review    Contract Signing
```

**Pricing Factors:**
1. **Seat Count:** Tiered pricing (1-50, 51-200, 201-500, 500+)
2. **Contract Term:** 12-month (15% discount), 24-month (25% discount)
3. **Deployment:** Cloud (standard) vs. On-premise (+40%)
4. **Support Level:** Standard vs. Premium (+20%)
5. **Custom Features:** Development hours billed separately

**Typical Enterprise Deal Size:**
- Small enterprise (50 users): $2,000-3,500/month
- Medium enterprise (200 users): $8,000-12,000/month  
- Large enterprise (500+ users): $20,000+/month

### 2.3 Implementation and Onboarding Process

**Week 1: Foundation**
- Dedicated implementation manager assigned
- Infrastructure setup (SSO, network configuration)
- Admin user training (2 hours)
- Initial user import and group setup

**Week 2: Configuration**
- Custom branding application
- Template customization workshop
- Integration setup (Jira, GitHub, etc.)
- Workflow configuration

**Week 3: Rollout**
- End-user training sessions (3 x 1-hour sessions)
- Communication plan execution
- Support channels established
- Feedback collection

**Week 4: Optimization**
- Usage review and optimization
- Advanced feature training
- Success metrics established
- Handoff to account management

### 2.4 Support and SLA Requirements

**Support Tiers:**
- **Standard Support:** 24-hour response, business hours
- **Premium Support:** 4-hour response, 24/7 coverage
- **Enterprise Support:** 1-hour response, dedicated engineer

**SLA Commitments:**
- **Uptime:** 99.9% (Standard), 99.95% (Premium), 99.99% (Enterprise)
- **Response Times:** As per support tier
- **Resolution Times:** 
  - P1 (Critical): 4 hours (Enterprise), 8 hours (Premium)
  - P2 (High): 8 hours (Enterprise), 24 hours (Premium)
  - P3 (Medium): 24 hours (Enterprise), 48 hours (Premium)
  - P4 (Low): 48 hours (Enterprise), 5 days (Premium)

**Support Channels:**
- Email (all plans)
- Chat (Pro and Enterprise)
- Phone (Enterprise only)
- Dedicated Slack channel (Enterprise)

### 2.5 Contract and Legal Considerations

**Standard Contract Terms:**
- **Term:** 12-month minimum for enterprise
- **Payment:** Annual upfront (discounted) or quarterly
- **Renewal:** Automatic with 60-day notice for cancellation
- **Price Protection:** 5% annual cap on price increases

**Legal Requirements:**
- **DPA:** Standard Data Processing Agreement
- **Security:** SOC 2 report available under NDA
- **Insurance:** $5M cyber liability insurance
- **Indemnification:** Mutual indemnification for IP infringement

**Negotiation Points:**
- Termination for convenience (typically 30-day notice)
- Price lock periods (2-3 years)
- Custom SLAs with penalties
- Data export requirements upon termination

## 12. CONCLUSION & RECOMMENDATIONS

### 12.1 Key Recommendations

1. **Start Simple:** Launch with Free and Starter plans first, validate pricing before adding complexity.

2. **Focus on Conversion:** Optimize the free-to-paid conversion path before expanding to enterprise.

3. **Build in Upsell Triggers:** Design feature limitations that naturally drive upgrades at the right time.

4. **Enterprise Readiness:** Don't build enterprise features until you have enterprise demand.

5. **Monitor Religiously:** Track all KPIs from day one to inform pricing and feature decisions.

6. **Stay Flexible:** Be prepared to adjust pricing based on market feedback and competitive pressure.

7. **Value Communication:** Clearly articulate the value at each price point, especially for enterprise.
